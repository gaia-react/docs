This section covers GAIA surface that exists in the GAIA repo but is not included in the `npx create-gaia` bundle.

Audience: the GAIA maintainer and any outside contributors working on GAIA itself.

<Aside type="caution">
If you ran `npx create-gaia my-app` for a scaffolded project, this section is not for you. Start with the [Quick Start](/) instead.
</Aside>

## Project / contributor split

GAIA routes its surface to two audiences. Two mechanisms strip contributor-only material from the release bundle:

1. **Path-level exclusion.** Files and directories listed in `.gaia/release-exclude` never enter the bundle the release workflow builds. Examples: `.claude/commands/gaia-release.md`, `.claude/commands/distribution-vet.md`, `.claude/skills/release-notes`, the two `code-audit-maintainer-*` agents, `.claude/rules/maintainers/`, `.gaia/cli/src/`, `.gaia/tests/`, `.gaia/scripts/tests/`, `.githooks/pre-push`, `wiki/entities/`, and `wiki/meta/`.
2. **Block-level exclusion.** Content between `<!-- gaia:maintainer-only:start -->` and `<!-- gaia:maintainer-only:end -->` markers inside files that do ship. The shipped file is a subset of the source.

Both pass through the bundle-time scrub during release. `.gaia/release-exclude` is the authoritative manifest of path-level rules, and each of its categories carries the reason its paths are withheld.

## What's in this section

- [CI workflows](/contributors/ci/): `release.yml`, `cli-tests.yml`, `audit-ci-tests.yml`, `shell-lint.yml`, `forensics-triage.yml`.
- [CLI surface](/contributors/cli/): `gaia` binary subcommands, the build layout, and where their source lives.
- [`/gaia-release`](/contributors/release/): audience, scope, and what releases produce. The detailed runbook is internal to the maintainer.
- [Contributor wiki content](/contributors/wiki/): `wiki/entities/`, `wiki/meta/`, and the `gaia:maintainer-only` block convention.

Beyond these pages, the GAIA repo carries maintainer-only tooling that is not documented here: the `/distribution-vet` ship-or-withhold vet, the `release-notes` skill, the two maintainer members of the Code Audit Team, and the verification runner under `.gaia/tests/`. Their source and headers are the reference.

## Before a release

`/health-audit` is removed in 2.0.0. The pre-release health check is [`/gaia-fitness`](/maintenance/fitness/) followed by [`/gaia-cleanse`](/maintenance/cleanse/), the same commands every project runs.

## Source-of-truth pointers

- `.gaia/release-exclude`: the path-level exclusion manifest
- `.gaia/cli/src/`: CLI source
- `wiki/entities/`, `wiki/meta/`: contributor wiki content
- `.github/workflows/`: CI workflows (a mix of contributor-only and shipped)
